🌑 SCENE 5-1｜Meme Perpetrator: Tech_Shadow_A (Character Design)
Character Code: Tech_Shadow_A  
= Quiet IT madness × Resentment toward the protagonist × Anonymous violence × The shadow of technology

● Surface (Quiet Layer)
Silent
Serious
Faint presence
Holds log management and access privileges
Resents the protagonist after the internal whistleblowing incident
Appears to be “a quiet person”

● Hidden Side (Flame Layer)
Operates multiple anonymous SNS accounts
Skilled in AI synthesis, clipping, and meme editing
Illicitly edits the protagonist’s voice, face, and statements from his radio success
Creates defamatory memes “for fun”
Gains ad revenue and feels a rush
Believes he is influencing the world
His mind quietly begins to fracture

● Core of the Character
A man who believes “anonymity is a safe zone.”
That is why the fall hits him the hardest.
