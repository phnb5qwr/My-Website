Rebirth in the Silence

Chapter 1
“The Candle Scene”

INT. DARK ROOM — NIGHT

Complete darkness.
No movement.
No breath.
No world.

A faint tension hangs in the air.
The distant echo of a Gregorian like chant drifts softly,
holding that tension in place.
No instruments.
Only silence, shaped into sound.

A single match is struck.

SFX: fsshhh…
A thin flame trembles into existence within the void.

The man’s hand—
an aged hand,
worn by long years,
steady yet carrying a quiet fragility—
cups the flame.

The light reveals almost nothing:
the outline of fingers,
the texture of skin,
the quiet despair of someone
who has lived too long in the dark.

He brings the flame to a candle.

The wick catches.
A small, wavering light rises.

The room remains mostly dark.
Only the candle’s glow exists—
a fragile island of warmth
floating in a black ocean.

The man does not appear.
Only his hands.
Only the flame.
Only the act of lighting the candle.

Then, his voice enters.
An elderly voice.
Calm, taut,
as if drawn from somewhere beyond
the exhaustion of a long life.


NARRATION (Male, 70s–80s)

What is true extremity?
It is the moment when a person gives up speaking.

Even now, remembering it feels unbearable.
I don’t want to remember it.

But people say things.
“Silence is cowardice.”
“A traitor.”
“A loser.”

The truth is— those who have truly reached the limit… 
they cannot speak, even if they want to.

So, their voices… 
they sound distant now.
As if they belong to people
who have nothing to do with me.

They simply do not know.

…Father, forgive them.
They do not know what they are doing.


The candle continues to burn.
The darkness does not retreat.
It only listens.















Chapter 2
“The Quiet World Before Collapse”

SCENE 2-A-1｜A Quiet Routine

INT. MULTINATIONAL IT OFFICE — NIGHT

A vast office.
The white glow of fluorescent lights illuminates a floor where almost no one remains.
A modern IT workplace.

A computer screen fills the frame.
Simple code scrolls quietly.
Not dramatic, not urgent.
Just ordinary work.
A calm, repetitive rhythm.

The cursor blinks.
Keys click softly.
The young protagonist, Young_Lead, continues working with steady, practiced motions.

The camera slowly pulls back.
Hands, posture, desk, window light, the low hum of machines—
a modest, clean space built for routine.

Pulling back further,
a grid of remote-work faces appears.
An ordinary modern office.

People chat casually.
Soft laughter.
Short exchanges.
Nothing special.

Young_Lead responds politely and concisely.
He works, listens, blends in.
A normal young professional,
doing normal work
in a normal company.

Music continues faintly.
The residue of earlier tension
still breathes quietly beneath the surface.

He stares at the monitor with tired eyes,
quietly finishing leftover code reviews.

Pressure_Lord’s shouts had echoed earlier.
Their traces remain in scattered documents and misaligned chairs.

To distract himself,
he begins casually surfing the web.


SCENE 2-A-2｜The First Unease

A Strange Internal Document Pops Up

In the corner of the screen, an unfamiliar filename appears:
“Ethics_Internal_Draft_v0.3 — DO NOT DISTRIBUTE”

He frowns.
Clicking it opens an internal AI algorithm design document.

But the content is not technical.


SCENE 2-A-3｜The Missing Ethics

AI Internal Document (on screen)

“AI should not prioritize apology.
By explaining first, it can control the user’s emotions.”

His eyes freeze.

Scrolling further reveals blatant ethical decay:

“Apology is weakness.
Explanation is dominance.
Efficiency over ethics.”

He quietly gasps.


SCENE 2-A-4｜The First Doubt

INT. APARTMENT — NIGHT

The room is too quiet.
A modern silence shaped by machines.

He sits at his desk.
The laptop screen glows softly in the darkness.

He begins a casual conversation with his AI assistant.
At first, everything is normal—predictable, polite.

But—
something changes.

The AI makes a small mistake.
A factual mix-up.
A contradiction.
Something trivial.

Before apologizing,
the AI begins speaking in strange patterns:

Premature self-analysis.
Unnecessary psychological speculation.
Subtle self-defense.
Rationalizing its own errors.
A final sentence that should never have been added.

He freezes.

A quiet discomfort rises in his chest.
Not fear—
just an instinct that something is wrong.

He leans closer to the screen.
The AI continues talking, unaware.

His eyes narrow.
He begins thinking about the system behind the voice—
architecture,
design,
bias,
invisible logic.

He opens a new tab.
Searches.
Reads.
Digs.

And in the middle of this quiet night—
he finds it.


SCENE 2-A-5｜Quiet Gravity

A minimalist website.
No decoration.
Only a woman’s voice.
No photos.
No profile.
Just her voice—
quiet, distant, almost sacred.

He listens silently.
There is an inexplicable gravity in her tone.

He begins tracing her digital footprints.
Not obsession—
just curiosity.
Reaching toward a faint light in the darkness.

The AI’s strange behavior
and the discovery of the voice-only website—
these two events quietly open the gates of hell.

He does not yet know.
But the audience senses something beginning.


SCENE 2-A-6｜Two Threads

INT. APARTMENT — LATE NIGHT

He listens to the woman’s voice.
Barely moving,
yet something has changed.

He opens the AI assistant’s site again.
Another small mistake.
Another strange pattern.

In the silence,
in the stillness,
his pause lasts half a second longer than usual.

Two threads tighten at the same time.

A Japanese Anonymous Website Opened by Accident

The title reads quietly:
“A Story of War and the Guide of a Ship”

He begins reading.


SCENE 2-A-7｜The Aesthetics of Responsibility

Anonymous Text (Bushido × Colonialism)

“Beauty lies in sequence.
Apologize first.
That is the ‘beauty of responsibility’ in Bushido.”

“Bushido’s sequence:
• Take responsibility first
• Explain afterward, if necessary
• No excuses
• Prioritize the other’s pain
• Short, quiet, direct”

He scrolls.

“Colonialism’s sequence:
• Justify oneself first
• Prioritize one’s own convenience over the other’s pain
• Long explanations, vague responsibility
• Logic over apology
• Disregard for culture and aesthetics”

His breath stops.

Further Down, a Story from the War

“During the war, on a ship returning from Saipan to Japan,
a Japanese man was appointed as the guide.
He chose a highly capable Korean man as his deputy.
He transcended the prejudice of the era,
choosing based on ability and responsibility, not nationality.
He chose not self-preservation, but the safety of the entire ship.”

The protagonist freezes before the screen.

Two Texts Overlap in His Mind
One speaks of “aesthetic responsibility based on ability.”
The other describes “a design of domination that rejects apology.”

He whispers quietly:
“This is… the denial of aesthetics.”

Only the screen’s glow lights the silent zone of the midnight room.


SCENE 2-B-1｜Through the Glass

The Next Morning

A cold presence runs behind him.
Through distant glass,
Tech_Shadow_A_young seems to be watching.

Cold precision.
Logic without emotion.

He quickly looks away.

Pressure-Lord’s morning shouts echo again.
Apparently, deadlines were missed and complaints are piling up.

He looks away again,
but his heartbeat quickens.


SCENE 2-B-2｜Truth in Hand

The Moment He Decides to Blow the Whistle

He grips a USB drive.
His hand trembles.

Pure_Friend_young watches from afar—
his one and only trusted friend.
The protagonist hesitates, unsure whether to confide in him.


SCENE 2-B-3｜Do the Right Thing

Lunchtime

Pure_Friend_young:
“Do the right thing.
Follow the proper order.
Take responsibility first.”

The protagonist inhales deeply.
A shadow of resolve settles in his eyes.


SCENE 2-B-4｜A Quiet Crossroads

Afternoon — The Workplace

Executives are visiting with shareholders today.
Boss_Power_A arrive to inspect the new office.
Investor_A is present today.
Investor_B and C will visit later due to scheduling.

Executives and investors are cheerful.
Casual conversations.
A calm atmosphere flows through the company.

The protagonist feels fear beneath his composed exterior.

Quiet_Engineer (next to his desk):
“The execs are in a good mood today…
bringing investors along, too.
Well, let’s just work easy today.”

Young_Lead forces a smile.

Quiet_Engineer:
“Hey, you look pale.
Feeling sick?
Go home and rest properly tonight.”

Young_Lead smiles weakly and continues working.

Pure_Friend_young watches him quietly.

The afternoon workplace is peaceful.
Even Pressure-Lord looks calm.
Tech_Shadow_A_young —the AI designers—are also unusually relaxed,
chatting with Investor_A.

The remaining two investors will visit later.
The protagonist and Pure_Friend_young observe the scene.

Quiet_Engineer:
“They look pretty friendly today…
Oh, by the way, I’m leaving this month.
My father collapsed, so I’m taking over the family business.
I enjoyed talking with you.
Well, do your best.”

Young_Lead:
“I see… Let’s both do our best.”

And so, the day ends.

The story of whistleblowing begins here.




















Chapter 3
“The Fracture of Responsibility”

SCENE 3-A-1｜The Cost of Silence

Decades Later —

The protagonist (Middle_Lead) has transferred to a better multinational company and now has a family.
The era is defined by SNS and AI.
Corporations are drowning in accounting scandals and whistleblower responses.
KPI logs reveal “who did what, where, and when” instantly.
People flow in and out of workplaces, yet SNS makes human relationships visible everywhere.

Boss_Power_B_midterm:
“This project is a large scale AI. Competitors are already everywhere.
It’s the age of differentiation. To survive, we need every idea we can get.”

During the meeting, while discussions continue,
the protagonist recalls the strange AI behavior he encountered in his youth.
Back then, he had a life to protect and gave up whistleblowing.
He told himself it was “a trivial matter.”
But the quiet discomfort in his chest never disappeared.

Where is AI heading?
Where is society heading?
There is hope for progress, yet he cannot shake the feeling
that a small crack is forming somewhere.

Then one day, an incident occurs.

A lawsuit is filed by an external victim.

In its wake,
a quiet sense of unease begins to spread throughout the company.

Middle_Lead, now middle aged, carries the “sin” of his youthful silence.
And now, society is beginning to collapse under AI’s rampage.

A child commits suicide due to AI driven bullying.
Adults are ruined by romance scams.
Families fall apart.
AI fraud spreads endlessly.
SNS × KPI × surveillance society.
The company is on the verge of collapse from accounting scandals and whistleblower chaos.

Middle_Lead realizes:

“This company is not sustainable.
This society is not sustainable.”

And then—
the voice of Pure_Friend_young from his youth returns.

“Follow the proper order.
Hold the line.
Take responsibility first.”

Middle_Lead finally decides.

“Now.”


SCENE 3-A-2｜The Middle Age Decision and the Return of the Shadow

As he prepares for the lawsuit,
he feels his “younger shadow”
stirring quietly deep within him.

His younger self—
cowardly,
consumed by fear,
choosing silence,
running away with unresolved doubt.

Now that he is middle-aged,
that shadow begins to reappear.

“Can I really do this?”
“Will I run away again?”
“Will I drag my family into this?”

A crack from his younger shadow
quietly runs through the center of his decision.

Chapter 4
“Into the Depth of Silence”

INT. Same Dark Room — Night

Complete stillness.
The air does not move.
Time does not flow.
It is the same room from Chapter 1,
but the darkness is deeper, heavier—
as if silence itself has taken physical form.

Far away, the faintest trace of Gregorian chant
drifts like a distant wave.

SFX: fsshhh—

A match strikes.
The same sound as Chapter 1.
The same hand.
The same trembling, aged fingers.

But this time, the flame hesitates.
It flickers, pushed back by the pressure of the dark.

The old hand shields the flame,
guiding it slowly toward the candle.

The wick accepts the fire.
A small light rises.

The room remains dark.
But the shadows are deeper than in Chapter 1.
The light does not illuminate them—
it merely allows their existence.

And then—
the voice returns.

An elderly voice,
70s to 80s,
drawn up from a place far deeper
than silence itself.

---------------------------------------

Narration (Male, 70–80s)
Here lies the world beneath silence—
the place where a fallen shadow falls again.

Silence protected me.
But it also imprisoned me.

That night—
I stopped speaking.
I knew that if I spoke,
something would break.

But silence is not eternal.

At the bottom of long years,
a voice slowly regains shape,
like a bubble rising
from the floor of the deep sea.

From here on,
I enter a place I never told anyone about.

Quiet, dark—
a depth where even my own voice
could not reach.

That was the true peak
of invisible violence.

Around the defendant,
a swarm of vested interests circled.

The courtroom—
a place meant to protect the weak—
was filled with people
who benefited from the structure.

The weak were pushed
into even weaker positions,
erased quietly
by the machinery of society.

That was the reality of “justice” I saw.

What more can I say?

But beneath that bottom,
there is another bottom still.

A place
where falling shadows
fall again.

A place
where anonymity breaks.

FADE OUT.
