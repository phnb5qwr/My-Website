# International University Film Project Proposal  
## (University × Film × Public Cultural Institution × Canada × International University Film Festival)  

---

# 1. Project Overview  
This project aims to completely detach from the structural violence of the traditional film industry and build the world’s first film production and film festival system that integrates:

**University × Film × Public Cultural Institution (Civil Service Model)**

Instead of treating film as an “industry,” this project redefines film as **public culture**.  
A university and a film cultural institution jointly operate the **International University Film Festival (IUFF)**, creating new actors, new film culture, and multi‑generational participation.

# 5. Project Philosophy (Complete Version)

### 1. Multi-generational Co-creation (18–70+)  
Film is not only for the young.  
Elderly citizens, students, and working adults all participate as cultural contributors.

### 2. Discovery of New Actors  
Actors are selected based on talent and soul, not agency politics or industry constraints.

### 3. Zero Structural Violence in Film Production  
Eliminate political pressure, corporate factions, agency control, and industry exploitation.  
Film is treated as pure culture.

### 4. University × Film × Civil Service Model  
Film is redefined as public culture, forming a new civilizational model.

### 5. Creating Films That Protect the Soul  
Stories that rise from structural violence and preserve human dignity.

### 6. Film Culture for the SNS Era  
Students’ SNS skills spread films globally.

### 7. Social Value of Elderly Women’s Auditions  
Centering the voices of socially vulnerable groups within cultural creation.
