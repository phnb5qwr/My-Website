St. Mary's Cathedral, Tokyo

Winter cold was settling into the air during the Christmas season. Illuminations were glowing across the city, while a pale overcast sky brought rain that drifted in and out. Feeling the chill, I walked quietly along the gentle slope of the path.

Near the top of the hill, St. Mary's Cathedral, Tokyo came into view. The tall obelisk was already visible from the entrance, rising calmly toward the sky. I continued walking into the grounds.

At the gate, a soft illumination depicting the Nativity was glowing quietly. To the left, the church's quiet gift space was visible.

Walking further, the obelisk appeared directly ahead. Its overwhelming presence made my steps pause for a moment.

To the right stood the entrance to the cathedral. Stepping gently through the door, the interior of the cathedral slowly revealed itself on the left.

After climbing a few steps, I entered the worship space. The high ceiling and spacious quietness spread softly through the air. The beauty of the architecture was striking, and I stood still for a while, simply looking.

Soon, the sound of the pipe organ began to flow from deep within the cathedral. Its gentle resonance filled the space, and I remained there quietly, listening.

At the boundary between the sanctuary and the worship space, a Nativity display was placed gently on a waist‑high stand. Looking at its gentle figures, a faint smile rose within me. A soft sense of Christmas seemed to drift quietly through the air.

Various important objects and statues were placed throughout the cathedral. I walked slowly, taking time to look at each of them. The Pietà drew my gaze, and I stood there quietly for a while.

Eventually, I stepped out of the cathedral. Beyond the obelisk, the Lourdes Grotto and the Virgin Mary appeared quietly. In her white robe and blue sash, Mary's gentle figure stood in calm silence, and I paused again. A little farther away, a statue of Joseph stood quietly, and I looked at it for a moment before leaving the area.

In the gift space, cut-out signboards of Luce and the small puppy Santino were placed together. Their gentle expressions created a soft, peaceful scene. Rosaries and other items were arranged quietly throughout the space.

Afterward, I left St. Mary's Cathedral, Tokyo. Outside, the winter rain and wind remained cold. Yet while walking down the slope, the image of the Nativity display drifted softly through my mind. Despite the cold air, a quiet warmth lingered gently within my heart.
