Kanazawa Castle Park — Part 3

I continued walking toward Ninomaru Square, where Kanazawa Castle stands. By then, the cold, the strong wind, and the slanting snow had all started to feel almost irrelevant. All that mattered was reaching the top of the stone walls where the Hishi Yagura turret stands, in Ninomaru Square. At that point, the bad weather itself felt like the natural state of things. It was surprising how quickly one grew used to such conditions.

Even so, the path had turned slushy, so I moved forward carefully, watching my footing. This was a place where I needed to stay calm. Passing through Shinmaru Square, I eventually arrived at the first gate of Kahoku-mon.

When its imposing form suddenly appeared before me, I could not help but let out a quiet "Oh." On top of the gate rested a tiled roof covered in snow, and on either side, narrow openings called sama were set into the white walls. The combination of white plaster walls and stone walls was beautiful, and the snow-covered roofs stretching out ahead naturally drew my eyes along the route. I passed through the first gate of Kahoku-mon and continued walking along the white wall I had seen earlier.

Before long, the deck of an observation platform came into view. When I glanced around, I noticed that there were almost no people in the area. With such strong winds and heavy snow, perhaps that was only to be expected. Or maybe it simply happened to be the time of day when most of the tourists had already left.

On the way up to the observation platform, I saw a sign that read "Kahoku-mon Second Gate Entrance," with a red arrow pointing up and to the left. It seemed that the direction of the arrow indicated the location of the second gate of Kahoku-mon. I climbed the wooden stairs and headed toward the observation deck. There was also a gently sloping ramp there, allowing anyone to enjoy the view.

The Hishi Yagura turret appeared even closer, its form blending beautifully with the snowy landscape. It was the perfect spot for taking photos, so I stopped and captured the scene. With almost no one else around, I was able to take my time and savor the view. The wind was still strong and the air was cold, yet somehow even that began to feel pleasant.

In any case, I was almost there. Feeling a growing sense of excitement, I continued walking. I descended from the observation platform and headed in the direction of the Hishi Yagura. Passing through Sannomaru Square, I made my way toward the inner moat.

A spectacular view opened up before me. It was breathtaking. The imposing Gojukken Nagaya and the Hishi Yagura stood in a dignified line along the inner moat. This was exactly the kind of place where one ought to take photos, I thought, and I pressed the shutter again and again.

Just then, a patch of blue sky appeared between the clouds, and the snowfall briefly came to a halt. Even so, a solid layer of snow remained on the roofs of Gojukken Nagaya and the Hishi Yagura. After walking all that way in such bad weather, the moment felt almost like a miracle.

When I took photos from a different angle, the sun slipped behind the clouds, and the surface of the inner moat, the stone walls, and the white-walled Gojukken Nagaya and Hishi Yagura above them all seemed to float quietly into view. The instant I saw that scene, I felt from the bottom of my heart that it had truly been worth walking all that way.

And so, I ended this part of the walk there. Next time, I will begin from Hashizume-mon, reached after passing through the second gate of Kahoku-mon.

金沢城公園 ― Part 3

金沢城のある二の丸広場方面へと向かっていく。 もう寒さとか強風とか斜め積雪とか、どうでもよくなってきた。 ともかく目指すのは、あの菱櫓が建つ城壁の上、二の丸広場だ。 ここまで来ると、この悪天候が当たり前のように感じられてくる。 慣れとは恐ろしいものだ。

とはいえ、歩道はみぞれ状になっているので、足元には気をつけながら歩を進める。 ここは冷静になるべきところだ。 新丸広場を通り過ぎ、河北門 一の門へとたどり着く。

ひょいと姿を現した堂々とした門構えに、おおっと声が漏れる。 門の上には雪をかぶった本瓦葺の屋根があり、両脇の白い壁には狭間（さま）が施されている。 白壁と石垣の組み合わせが美しく、正面には雪をまとった屋根が続き、順路が自然と目に入る。 そのまま河北門 一の門をくぐり抜け、先ほどの白壁に沿って歩いていく。

途中で展望台のデッキが見えてきた。 ふと周りを見渡すと、この辺りはほとんど人がいない。 強風と積雪なのでそんなものだろう。 または、ほとんどの観光客がちょうど去った時間帯だったからかもしれない。

展望台へ上る途中の看板には「河北門 二の門入口」と書かれ、赤い矢印が左上を指している。 どうやら矢印の方向が河北門 二の門らしい。 木製の階段を上り、展望台へ向かう。 ここにはスロープもあり、誰でも景色を楽しめるようになっている。

菱櫓がさらに近くなり、雪景色と溶け合うように美しい姿を見せていた。 ちょうど良い撮影スポットだったので写真を撮る。 ほとんど人がいなかったので、ゆっくりと景色を味わうことができた。 相変わらず強風で寒いが、それすらも心地よく感じてくる。

ともかく、もうすぐだ。 ワクワクしながら歩を進める。 展望台を降り、菱櫓の方面へ向かっていく。 三の丸広場を抜け、内堀へと向かう。

絶景が広がっていた。 これは凄い。 圧巻の五十間長屋と菱櫓が、内堀沿いに堂々と続いている。 これこそ写真を撮るべき場所だと思い、夢中でシャッターを切る。 ちょうど雲の間から青空がのぞき、積雪も一時的に止んでいた。

五十間長屋と菱櫓の屋根にはしっかりと雪が残っている。 悪天候の中ここまで歩いてきた自分には、この瞬間が奇跡のように思えた。

別角度から写真を撮ると、太陽が雲に隠れ、内堀の水面と石垣、その上に建つ白壁の五十間長屋と菱櫓が静かに浮かび上がっていた。 その光景を見た瞬間、本当にここまで歩いてきてよかったと心の底から思った。
